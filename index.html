Google Drive sync

Not connected

Connect a Google account and your record is kept in a private folder in your own Drive — it survives clearing your browser, and any device you sign in on picks up where you left off. The app can only see the one file it creates; it has no access to the rest of your Drive.

Data & privacy

Everything you type is written to this browser's local storage. With Google Drive sync switched off the page makes no network requests at all; switch it on and the only thing sent anywhere is your own record, to your own Drive. The page itself is published publicly, so anyone opening the link sees an empty dashboard, never your entries.

To move your record between devices, use Backup to download a JSON file, keep it somewhere private — email it to yourself, or drop it in iCloud or Drive — and load it with Restore. Never commit a backup into a public repo: the file is your actual record, unlike this page.
